SEV1: customer-facing outage or data loss risk: page immediately, open an incident channel, update status page every 30 minutes. SEV2: degraded or a single region impaired: page, update hourly.
SEV3: internal tooling or no customer impact: ticket, next business day. Write a postmortem for SEV1 and SEV2 within five working days; blameless, with timeline and follow-up owners.
